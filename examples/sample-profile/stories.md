# Stories

## st-01 · role-northwind-ba · early 2025

**Prompt:** What did people come to you for?
**Story:** The account managers kept asking me why the account screen had so many boxes. We sat down with three of them, went through a normal day, and cut the record back to the fields that day actually needed.
**Who it was for:** The account managers.
**What changed for them:** A clear order of work and much less to type.
**What it did for the organisation:** Fewer duplicate accounts and fewer mistakes; they came back asking for the next round of changes.
**How someone could check:** The change request in the team backlog, and the before and after field lists.
**Confirmed:** 2026-01-11

## st-02 · role-northwind-ba · spring 2025

**Prompt:** The ads ask about running workshops. Was there a time you got people in a room to agree something?
**Story:** Sales and finance used different words for the same things. I ran a couple of sessions to agree the terms and what each team needed before anything was built.
**Who it was for:** The sales and finance leads, and the build team.
**What changed for them:** One agreed set of terms and requirements.
**What it did for the organisation:** The build worked from one specification.
**How someone could check:** The workshop notes and the signed-off specification.
**Confirmed:** 2026-01-11

## st-03 · role-contoso-support · 2023

**Prompt:** Something that broke, and what you did.
**Story:** The same questions kept coming in as tickets. I rewrote the how-to guides around the questions people actually asked.
**Who it was for:** Users, and the newer people on the support desk.
**What changed for them:** Users found answers without raising a ticket; new starters could answer without escalating.
**What it did for the organisation:** Fewer repeat tickets on those topics.
**How someone could check:** The ticket topic report.
**Confirmed:** 2026-01-11

## st-04 · role-contoso-support · 2022

**Prompt:** A normal week.
