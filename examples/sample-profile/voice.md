# Voice

## Standard

Plain and specific. Say what changed for people first, then what it did for the business. No big words.

## Samples

When I joined, the account managers were filling in thirty fields to log a call, and most of them didn't matter to anyone. We sat down with three of them, walked through what they actually did in a day, and cut the record back to what that day needed.

## Keep

- 2026-01-11: "Gave account managers a clear order of work": because that's what they said to me.

## Cut

- 2026-01-11: "Spearheaded a transformative data initiative": I wouldn't say that out loud.
