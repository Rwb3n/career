Customer Support Advisor
Northwind Home Delivery, Sheffield (hybrid, 2 days in office)
GBP 25,000 to 27,000

About the role
You'll be the first point of contact for customers by phone, email and chat, sorting out delivery problems, refunds and complaints. You'll log every contact in our CRM and pass anything you can't fix to the right team.

What you'll do
- Handle customer queries and complaints, aiming to resolve them first time
- Process refunds and replacements within policy, and know when to escalate
- Keep accurate records of every contact
- Spot repeat problems and tell your team lead

What we're looking for
- Experience in a customer-facing role (retail, hospitality or contact centre)
- Calm with upset customers
- Comfortable with computers; CRM experience a plus
- Good written English

No experience in a contact centre needed: we train you.
